About

What is this platform?

The MBE Solver is a web-based reservoir engineering application built to help engineers and students solve material balance calculations quickly and accurately, without the overhead of manual computation.

Material Balance Equation analysis is one of the most important techniques in reservoir engineering — used to estimate hydrocarbon reserves, evaluate reservoir performance, and understand drive mechanisms. This platform centralises those calculations in one place.
