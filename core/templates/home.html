Welcome to the Material Balance Equation Solver

A Petroleum Reservoir Engineering Calculation Platform

Welcome to the Material Balance Equation (MBE) Solver, a web-based petroleum engineering application developed to assist students, researchers, and reservoir engineers in performing reservoir engineering calculations quickly and accurately.

Material Balance Equation analysis is one of the most important techniques used in reservoir engineering for estimating hydrocarbon reserves, evaluating reservoir performance, and understanding reservoir drive mechanisms. Traditionally, these calculations are performed manually, which can be time-consuming and prone to computational errors.

This software provides a centralized platform for solving important reservoir engineering equations including:

  • General Material Balance Equation (MBE)
  • Underground Withdrawal Term (F)
  • Oil Expansion Term (Eo)
  • Gas Cap Expansion Term (Eg)
  • Formation and Water Expansion (Efw)
  • Gas Formation Volume Factor (Bg)
  • Gas Cap Ratio (m)
  • Original Oil In Place (OOIP)
  • Original Gas In Place (OGIP)
  • Recovery Factor (RF)
  • Schilthuis Water Influx Model
  • Van Everdingen-Hurst Water Influx Model
  • Fetkovich Water Influx Model

The primary goal of this project is to provide an easy-to-use digital tool that improves computational accuracy, reduces calculation time, and enhances understanding of reservoir behavior through practical engineering calculations.
